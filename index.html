Tutorials

How do I start?

Log in, choose a DEMO account and press Connect. Open the Bots tab, load a free bot, check the settings in the Run panel and press Run.

What do the settings mean?

Stake is the amount per trade. Duration is how many ticks a trade lasts. The bot stops by itself when your stop loss, take profit or maximum number of trades is reached.

Demo or real account?

A demo account uses virtual money. A real account uses your own money, and the site asks you to confirm before connecting and before running a bot.

Is profit guaranteed?

No. Every trade can lose its full stake and no bot guarantees profit. Bots only trade while this page stays open.
